💡 What happens during a pipeline test?

  1. Scoring: Your answer gets an initial quality score (0-100)
  2. Analysis: Each layer examines different aspects — claims, facts, weaknesses
  3. Adversarial: The pipeline actively tries to find flaws and contradictions
  4. Rewrite: A better version is generated based on all findings
  5. Scoring: The improved answer gets a new score — see the delta!
  6. Certification: Final verdict: CERTIFIED, CERTIFIED WITH CAUTION, or REQUIRES REVIEW
